One app to rule them all. Tired of juggling a different app for every LED strip? Universal LED Strip Remote controls your LED strips and smart bulbs, over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or infrared, from one simple app.

Free. No ads. No account. Open source. What more could you ask for?

★ All your lighting, one app
• Bluetooth LED strips: most cheap controllers (ELK-BLEDOM, ELK-BLEDOB, MELK…) work right away, no pairing code
• Wi-Fi lights with an open protocol: Philips Hue, Yeelight, LIFX, WLED, Govee (LAN control), Shelly, Tasmota, OpenBeken, Magic Home, Nanoleaf, Elgato
• Infrared LED strips: if your phone has an IR blaster, it becomes the remote, and the app finds the right codes by itself
The app scans around you on its own: tap a light to add it.

★ Colors, whites and effects
Any color, whites from warmest (candle) to coolest (daylight), brightness with a swipe along the strip, and your controller's built-in effects with their speed.

★ Ready-made moods
Christmas, Halloween, Autumn, Aurora, Romantic, Ocean, Miami, Forest: one tap and your light changes mood. On an addressable (pixel) strip they truly come alive (twinkling garlands, rippling aurora, falling leaves) and keep running with your phone off.

★ Create your own moods
Paint your strip LED by LED on a strip drawn in an S shape, or pick a style (bands, glows, garland, leaves…). Try it live on your strip, then keep it. Any mood from the app can be your starting point.

★ Rooms and widgets
Group your lights by room, switch a whole room in one tap or set its mood (Cozy, Relax, Fiesta, or your own). Widgets do all of this from your home screen.

★ Free and ad-free, for real
No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription, no sign-up. The code is open source (GPL-3.0) and public on GitHub. Nothing leaves your phone, and the app works without internet.

Questions
• Does it work with my light? If it shows up as ELK-BLEDOM, ELK-BLEDOB or MELK in a Bluetooth scan, speaks an open protocol over Wi-Fi, or came with a small infrared remote, yes.
• My phone has no IR blaster? Then remote-controlled lights can't be used (no app can do it without one), but everything else works.
• Tuya / Smart Life? These devices are locked by their maker: no other app can control them. The app recognizes them and tells you what you can do.
