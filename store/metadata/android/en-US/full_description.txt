One app to rule them all. No more juggling a different app for every LED strip: control — and even set up — your strips and smart bulbs over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or infrared, from one simple app.

No ads. No account. Open source.

💡 All your lights, one app
• Bluetooth: most budget controllers (ELK-BLEDOM, MELK…)
• Wi-Fi: Philips Hue, Yeelight, LIFX, WLED, Govee, Shelly, Tasmota, Magic Home, Nanoleaf…
• Infrared: your phone becomes the remote and finds the right codes on its own
The app looks around you: tap a light to add it.

📶 New Wi-Fi light? Ready in seconds
No setup web page, no IP address to type: the app spots Wi-Fi lights still waiting for your network (Tasmota, WLED, OpenBeken, ESPHome, Shelly, Magic Home…). Type your Wi-Fi password or scan its QR code, even from a screenshot, and the app does the rest.

🎨 Colors, whites and moods
Every color, warm to cool white, brightness with a swipe. Ready-made moods (Christmas, Halloween, Aurora, Ocean…), or create your own by painting your strip LED by LED.

🏠 Rooms and widgets
Turn on a whole room with one gesture, or set its mood right from your home screen.

🔒 Nothing leaves your phone
No ads, no purchases, no sign-up. Works without internet.

Tuya / Smart Life: these devices are locked by their maker. The app recognizes them and tells you what to do.

Universal LED Strip Remote is an independent app, not affiliated with the makers of these lights.
