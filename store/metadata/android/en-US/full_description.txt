Lost track of which app goes with which LED strip? Universal LED Strip Remote brings them all together in one simple app. Free, no ads, no account.

★ Bluetooth LED strips
Most cheap Bluetooth LED strip controllers sold online (named ELK-BLEDOM, ELK-BLEDOB or MELK) work directly:
• turn on and off
• any color, with quick colors and a white slider
• brightness
• built-in effects (fade, jump, rainbow, fire…) and their speed

★ Strips with an infrared remote
Lost the remote? If your phone has an IR blaster (many Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones do), it becomes the remote. The app finds the right codes by itself: tap the screen as soon as your strip reacts, that's it. Colors, brightness and effects then work like on the remote.
Tip: a phone's IR blaster reaches much less far than a remote. Hold the top of the phone within about 30 cm (1 ft) of the small box on the strip's cable.

★ Rooms
Group your strips by room, then turn a whole room on or off in one tap.

★ Shortcuts
Turn a room on or off from a home screen shortcut or an automation app, with a simple link.

★ Other controllers
The app recognizes many other controllers (Bluetooth and Wi-Fi) and tells you which app they come from. If one has an infrared remote, add it as infrared and control everything from here.

★ Private by design
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone. The app works without internet.

Note: Wi-Fi devices locked by their maker (Tuya / Smart Life) can't be controlled by any other app. Universal LED Strip Remote is an independent app, not affiliated with the makers of these controllers or their apps.
